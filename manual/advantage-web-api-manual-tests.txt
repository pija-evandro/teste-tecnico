    Then the searched product should be available in the results
    And the results should be relevant to the search

  Scenario: Search for an existing product by partial name
    When a customer searches using a meaningful part of a product name
    Then the results should contain products related to that term

  Scenario: Search with different letter casing
    When a customer searches for an existing product using different letter casing
    Then the expected products should still be found

  Scenario: Search for a nonexistent product

    Then unrelated products should not be presented
    And the customer should understand that no matching product was found

  Scenario: Search with leading and trailing spaces
    When a customer searches for an existing product with extra spaces
    Then the search should still return relevant results

  Scenario: Search using special characters
    When a customer searches using only special characters
    Then the search should be handled safely
    And unrelated products should not be presented


Feature: Advantage Online Shopping - Shopping cart

  Background:
    Given a customer has found an available product

  Scenario: Add one product to the cart
    When the customer adds the product to the cart
    Then the cart should contain the selected product with the correct purchase information

  Scenario: Add two different products to the cart
    Given one product is already in the cart
    When the customer adds another product
    Then both products should be represented correctly in the cart
    And the cart total should reflect both products

  Scenario: Add the same product more than once
    Given a product is already in the cart
    When the customer adds the same product again with the same variation
    Then the cart should represent the resulting quantity correctly
    And the amount charged for the product should remain consistent

  Scenario: Update product quantity
    Given a product is in the cart
    When the customer changes its quantity to another valid value
    Then the cart should reflect the new quantity
    And the amounts should be recalculated accordingly

  Scenario: Remove a product from the cart
    Given a product is in the cart
    When the customer removes that product
    Then it should no longer be included in the purchase
    And the cart totals should reflect the remaining items

  Scenario: Preserve the cart while browsing
    Given a customer has products in the cart
    When the customer continues browsing the catalog
    Then the previously selected products should remain in the cart


Feature: Advantage Online Shopping - Checkout

  Scenario: Review a single product before completing the purchase
    Given a customer has one product in the cart
    When the customer proceeds to checkout

    And the calculated amount should remain consistent

  Scenario: Review multiple products before completing the purchase
    Given a customer has multiple products in the cart
    When the customer proceeds to checkout
    Then all selected products should be included in the order review
    And their quantities, prices and total should remain consistent with the cart

  Scenario: Prevent checkout with an empty cart
    Given the customer has no products in the cart
    When the customer attempts to continue with the purchase
    Then an order without products should not be created
